<h2 id="anthropic">Anthropic supply chain risk</h2>
<p><a href="https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/25/pentagon-anthropic-ai-risk-appeals-court.html">CNBC</a> (Fri): The U.S. Court of Appeals for the D.C. Circuit upheld, 2–1, the Pentagon’s March designation of Anthropic as a supply chain risk. That label keeps Claude off Defense systems and blocks contractors from using it in DoD work. Judges Gregory Katsas and Neomi Rao wrote that the Department had “ample support” that continued Claude integration presented a covered national-security risk under the Supply Chain Security Act. Judge Karen LeCraft Henderson dissented. Anthropic had sued after talks over GenAI.mil collapsed: Defense wanted broad lawful use; Anthropic wanted limits on fully autonomous weapons and domestic mass surveillance. A San Francisco federal judge earlier knocked down a parallel designation; this ruling upholds the other track. The panel stayed immediate effect so Anthropic can seek rehearing or the Supreme Court. Anthropic says it respectfully disagrees and is considering all options.</p>
<p>Separately, <a href="https://the-decoder.com/white-house-tells-openai-and-anthropic-to-let-u-s-review-new-models-before-sharing-them-with-british-testers/">Politico / The Decoder</a> (Fri): the White House’s Office of the National Cyber Director asked OpenAI and Anthropic to hold new frontier models from the UK AI Security Institute until U.S. agencies review them first. Anthropic has already limited Claude Mythos 5.1 to U.S. organizations. AISI’s Henry de Zoete told Parliament the institute still has frontier access and tested GPT-6 Astra before release.</p>

<h2 id="copilot">Copilot Home, Code, Autopilot</h2>
<p><a href="https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/09/25/introducing-the-new-copilot-with-home-code-and-autopilot/">Microsoft</a> / <a href="https://www.theverge.com/news/1000532/microsoft-copilot-super-app-chat-coding-autopilot">The Verge</a> (Fri): Jared Spataro’s post ships a redesigned Copilot app with three modes. Home merges Chat and Cowork, with Office documents live inside the app. Code lets anyone describe an app, tracker, dashboard, or automation in natural language; it runs on GitHub Copilot tech in a sandbox and can host inside the tenant via Copilot Managed Runtime. Autopilot (formerly Scout) is a persistent cloud agent with its own identity, memory, computer, and workspace on Microsoft IQ. It lives in Teams and Outlook, watches channels, runs recurring work, and keeps going while you sleep. Home and Code roll to the Frontier program in coming weeks; Autopilot enters private preview end of month.</p>
<p>Pricing split matters: a user subscription license covers everyday Chat and Office Copilot with Auto model routing at fixed cost. Cowork, Code, Autopilot, and frontier models like Astra and Fable run on usage-based billing. FinOps for AI expands cost controls in Agent 365 for Code and Managed Runtime. Nadella calls Copilot a new OS for work. Bloomberg frames it as Microsoft stepping off the personal chatbot race and leaning into work.</p>

<h2 id="learn">Something to learn</h2>
<p>A supply chain risk designation is not a product review. Under the U.S. Supply Chain Security Act, when Defense labels a vendor a risk, the agency and its contractors generally cannot keep using that product in DoD systems. For Anthropic, that means Claude is off military stacks even if a civilian court knocks down a parallel paper trail. When you hear “blacklisted by the Pentagon,” ask which statute, which court track is still live, and whether contractors are covered the same way as the agency itself. The rehearing window and a possible Supreme Court petition are how these designations get flipped, not a press release.</p>
<p>Fixed license versus usage billing is how Microsoft is splitting “everyday Copilot” from “agents that keep working.” A user subscription license buys Chat and Office Copilot at a predictable seat cost, with Auto picking models. Cowork, Code, Autopilot, and frontier models like Astra and Fable meter on usage-based billing. That is why FinOps dashboards and credit approvals showed up in the same announcement. When a vendor says AI is included in the seat, ask which surfaces are seat-priced and which ones start the meter when the agent runs overnight.</p>
