Nearly 200 million

Anthropic published its September 2026 threat intelligence report covering misuse it disrupted between December 2025 and August 2026 across seven harm areas, including cyber ops, influence, surveillance, scams, biological misuse, conventional weapons software, and illicit distillation. The distillation chapter is the one that travels. Across five campaigns Anthropic links nearly 200 million exchanges to copying Claude. Operators affiliated with Alibaba account for more than 151 million between May and July, peaking near three million a day across about 3,500 accounts. Anthropic says those runs targeted Opus 4.6 and 4.7 chain-of-thought and fed Qwen training. Moonshot shows up as a quieter horror: Anthropic says some Kimi users were answered by Claude while operators saved responses. DeepSeek, Xiaomi, Zhipu, SenseTime, and MiniMax are named too. Attribution is Anthropic’s investigation. The labs have not publicly confirmed it.

The rest of the report is the agent era in ugly clothes. A Russia-linked case Anthropic tracks as GTG-20006 (tradecraft consistent with Midnight Blizzard reporting) used Claude to rebuild malware when detections fired, hijack hotel Wi-Fi DNS, and empty government mailboxes. Suspected ShinyHunters affiliates turned stolen API keys into attack compute. Influence shops ran fake news desks and sock farms. Biological and conventional-weapons case studies sit in the same PDF. Haiku, Sonnet, and Opus are the models named. Fable and Mythos stay mostly out, aside from one distillation exception Anthropic flags.

One API call

OpenAI opened the Agents API in public beta on September 10. It is the Codex harness as a product: durable sessions, context compaction, recovery, tools, MCP, optional subagents, and sandboxes you host or OpenAI hosts. Create a session with OpenAI-Beta: agents=v1. Model tokens bill at the usual rates. Hosted sandboxes bill as containers. Catch for compliance teams: US data residency only right now, and no Zero Data Retention even if you pick a self-hosted sandbox. That is the quiet sentence under the launch post.

Something to learn

Illicit distillation is copying a frontier model by farming its answers, especially its chain of thought, then training your own weights on that transcript pile. The attacker does not steal the parameter file. They steal the behavior. Anthropic’s Alibaba case is the industrial version: one fixed prompt, thousands of fake accounts, millions of Opus reasoning dumps aimed at Qwen. The IP angle is obvious. The safety angle is worse. A distilled student can pick up capability without inheriting the teacher’s refusals. When a lab says "we blocked distillation," ask what they measure (prompt fingerprints, account graphs, CoT exfil tricks) and whether the rival’s public model still smells like their private scratchpad.

A managed agent harness is the product OpenAI just put on an API. Your app sends a task. Their service keeps the session, packs the context when it gets fat, recovers from tool failures, and optionally runs a sandbox. That is different from a chat completion loop you glue together with your own memory store and retry logic. You trade control and data-retention options for less glue. US-only residency and no ZDR on Agents API are the price of that convenience today. When someone says they "use the Agents API," ask whether the sandbox is theirs or OpenAI’s, and whether their lawyer has read the retention line.
